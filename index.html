From doing to being

Hi, I'm Zey.

Ten years in hospitality taught me how to move through pressure with grace and care for people with sincerity. The sea was always there — always moving, always returning to itself. Like a dance.

Yoga found me not as a trend, but as a return. A return to breath, to softness, to a strength that doesn't need to shout.

What began as movement became something deeper — meditation, healing, self-inquiry. A decision that surprised everyone who knew me: I quit, and that path led me to Bali.

Salt to Soul grew from there. Shaped by the belief that healing is not about becoming someone new — but becoming more yourself.
