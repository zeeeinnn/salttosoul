A moment for yourself

Morning light.
Evening calm.

Breathe.

There is a moment, just after you step inside, when the sounds of Malé soften and something quieter takes their place. You remove your slippers. You find your mat. You have not yet moved, and already something has begun to change.

Salt to Soul was built around that moment. Not the postures, not the sequence, but the quality of presence that becomes possible when you give yourself permission to slow down.

This is not a studio. It is a practice. One you return to.

From doing to being

Hi, I'm Zey.

Maldivian, through and through.

For over ten years, I worked in the Maldivian hospitality industry, learning how to hold space for people, stay composed under pressure, and find meaning in the smallest gestures of care. The ocean was always close. Always moving. Always returning to itself.

Life brought its own teachings. Ones I did not choose but could not avoid. Through those years, I found my way back to stillness, learned to value solitude, and began to understand that slowing down is not a retreat from the world, but a deeper way of meeting it.

Movement became part of that journey. What began as a way to inhabit my body became a practice that helped me feel present, grounded, and at home in my own skin.

I trained in Bali, completed 200 hours of immersive teacher training, and came back to Malé with something I could not have planned for: a clear sense of what this work is for. Salt to Soul grew from that. Rooted in island life, shaped by the rhythm of the ocean, and offered to those who are ready to return to themselves.

The setting

Every experience unfolds in both spaces. Sunrise to sunset, rain or shine, your practice continues exactly as planned.

Indoors

Warm, candlelit, with natural light streaming through tall windows. Lavender bolsters, mats, and quiet that settles the moment you enter.
Outdoors
Under the Sky
An open terrace above the city. Sunrise flows at first light, sunset flows as the sky turns gold, moon gatherings with no ceiling but the stars.

Private journeys can move between both spaces, or settle in one. Everything is laid out and waiting when you arrive: mats, bolsters, and blocks. Reserve your space privately

What's waiting for you

What we carry
to every practice.

The small, intentional details that transform a session into something you carry with you long after the mat is rolled away.

01
Lavender
eye pillow

Placed gently over your eyes during Savasana. Weighted, warm, lightly scented. An invitation to fully release what you came in holding.

02
Chilled
towel

Frozen, folded, and waiting after practice. Pressed cool against the skin the moment you need it most. The kind of small detail you don't forget.

03
Herbal
tea

Poured and waiting after every session. Something warm to sit with, to breathe in, before you return to the world. Take your time.

04
Affirmation
card

A single sentence chosen each day and placed on your mat. Offered at the close. Something to carry with you. Yours to keep.
