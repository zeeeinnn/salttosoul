The teacher

Hi, I'm
Zey.

Maldivian · 200-hour certified · Founder of Salt to Soul

From doing to being

Working with me means
returning to yourself.

I didn't set out to become a yoga teacher. I set out to come home to myself. Teaching turned out to be the same thing.

For 12 years I worked in Maldivian hospitality — learning how to hold space for people, stay composed under pressure, and find meaning in the smallest gestures of care.

Movement found me at a moment of searching. What began as a way to inhabit my own body became a practice that helped me feel present, grounded, and genuinely at home in my own skin.

Yoga Union · Bali, 2026

Why Salt to Soul exists

I trained in Bali. I taught on a private island. I came back to Malé and built the practice I had always wanted to find — somewhere quiet to breathe in the middle of a very loud world.

Bali · 2026 · Soneva Secret, Maldives

The symbol

The spiral is the whole story.

The spiral is one of the oldest symbols humanity has ever drawn. It moves inward and outward — representing the winding journey we must take into ourselves to truly know who we are. Every time you step onto a mat, you begin that journey again. You return carrying something you didn't have before.

Salt — from the islands that shaped her. Soul — what the practice gives back.

Teaching philosophy

No performance
required.
Just show up.

Zey's approach is grounded in alignment and permission. Alignment — because understanding how the body is designed to move protects it and deepens the practice. Permission — because most people arrive already carrying a story about what they can't do.

In her sessions you will not be asked to perform. You will be invited to explore. To stay with sensation. To notice what happens when you stop running from stillness and let it arrive.

You don't need to look a certain way on a mat. You just need to show up.

What guides the practice

What Salt to Soul
stands for.

01
Accessibility

Every session is designed to be entered at any level. The person who has never done yoga and the person who has practiced for years are both welcome. Neither is more valid than the other.

02
Honesty

In the body, in the room, in the teaching. Zey guides from a place of presence, not performance. She teaches what she knows, acknowledges what she doesn't, and stays curious.

03
The Maldives

This practice belongs to these islands. Shaped by the ocean, the light, the heat, and the stillness you only find when water surrounds you on every side.
