The teacher

Hi, I'm Zey.

Maldivian · 200-hour certified · Founder of Salt to Soul

Movement is not something I do. It is the way I come back to myself.
Her story

Maldivian,
through and
through.

For 12 years, I worked in the Maldivian hospitality industry — learning how to hold space for people, stay composed under pressure, and find meaning in the smallest gestures of care. The ocean was always close. Always moving. Always returning to itself.

Life brought its own teachings. Ones I did not choose but could not avoid. Through those years, I found my way back to stillness, learned to value solitude, and began to understand that slowing down is not a retreat from the world — it is a deeper way of meeting it.

Movement became part of that journey. What began as a way to inhabit my body became a practice that helped me feel present, grounded, and at home in my own skin.

I trained in Bali. 200 hours of immersive teacher training — asana, philosophy, anatomy, breathwork, and the immense responsibility of guiding another person's practice. I came back to Malé with something I could not have planned for: a clear sense of what this work is for.

Salt to Soul grew from that. Rooted in island life, shaped by the rhythm of the ocean, and offered to those who are ready to return to themselves.

Teaching philosophy

No performance
required.

Zey's approach to teaching is grounded in two things: alignment and permission. Alignment — because understanding how the body is designed to move protects it and deepens the practice. Permission — because most people arrive on a mat already carrying a story about what they can't do.

In her sessions, you will not be asked to perform. You will be invited to explore. To stay with sensation. To notice what happens when you stop running from stillness and simply let it arrive.

This applies equally to yoga and dance meditation. In movement, there is no right or wrong. There is only what is honest.

You don't need to look a certain way on a mat. You just need to show up.
What guides the practice

What Salt to Soul
stands for.

01
Accessibility

Every session is designed to be entered at any level. The person who has never done yoga and the person who has practiced for years are both welcome. Neither is more valid than the other.

02
Honesty

In the body, in the room, in the teaching. Zey guides from a place of presence, not performance. She teaches what she knows, acknowledges what she doesn't, and stays curious.

03
The Maldives

This practice belongs to these islands. It is shaped by the ocean, the light, the heat, and the particular stillness you only find when you're surrounded by water on every side.
