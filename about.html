The teacher

Hi,

Zey.

From doing to being

I didn't plan to become a yoga teacher. I planned to come back to myself. Teaching turned out to be the same thing.

For 12 years I worked in Maldivian hospitality — learning how to hold space for people, stay composed under pressure, and find meaning in the smallest gestures of care. The ocean was always close. Always moving. Always returning to itself.

Life brought its own teachings. Ones I did not choose but could not avoid. Through those years I found my way back to stillness — and began to understand that slowing down is not a retreat from the world. It is a deeper way of meeting it.

The symbol

The spiral is
the whole story.

The spiral is one of the oldest symbols humanity has ever drawn. It moves inward and outward — representing the winding journey we must take into ourselves if we are to truly know who we are.

Every time you step onto a mat, you begin that journey again. You spiral a little deeper. You return carrying something you didn't have before — more stillness, more presence, more of yourself.

Salt — from the islands that shaped her. Soul — what the practice gives back.

The practice she had always wanted to find. Sunrise yoga on the rooftop. Full moon gatherings. Private sessions. Dance meditation. All of it rooted in these islands, offered with full presence and genuine care.

Teaching philosophy

No performance required.
Just show up.

Zey's approach is grounded in alignment and permission. Alignment — because understanding how the body is designed to move protects it. Permission — because most people arrive carrying a story about what they can't do.

In her sessions you will not be asked to perform. You will be invited to explore. To stay with sensation. To notice what happens when you stop running from stillness and let it arrive.

What guides the practice

What Salt to Soul
stands for.

01
Accessibility

Every session is designed to be entered at any level. The person who has never done yoga and the person who has practiced for years are both welcome. Neither is more valid than the other.

02
Honesty

In the body, in the room, in the teaching. Zey guides from presence, not performance. She teaches what she knows, acknowledges what she doesn't, and stays curious.

03
The Maldives

This practice belongs to these islands. Shaped by the ocean, the light, the heat, and the stillness you only find when water surrounds you on every side.
