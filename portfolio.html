Who she is

Rooted in
these islands.

Zey is a Maldivian yoga teacher based in Malé. Before she taught, she spent 12 years in the hospitality industry. Learning how to hold space, stay composed under pressure, and find meaning in the smallest gestures of care. That background shapes everything about the way she works.

She trained at Yoga Union in Bali in 2026, completing 200 hours of immersive residential teacher training. She returned to the Maldives and built Salt to Soul, a practice rooted in island life, shaped by the rhythm of the ocean.

In the same year, she spent two weeks as a visiting yoga teacher at Soneva Secret, one of the Maldives' most exclusive private island resorts. Daily sessions overlooking the Indian Ocean, and the practised ability to meet guests exactly where they are.

The details

What every session
includes.

Small, intentional touches that turn a yoga session into something guests carry home. She thinks in experiences, not just classes.

01
Lavender
eye pillow

Placed gently during Savasana. Weighted, warm, lightly scented. An invitation to fully release what the guest came in holding.

02
Chilled
towel

Frozen, folded, and waiting after practice. Pressed cool against the skin at exactly the right moment. The kind of detail guests mention first.

03
Iced
tea

Poured and waiting after every session. Something to sit with, to breathe over, before returning to the day.

04
Affirmation
card

A single sentence, chosen and placed on the mat. Offered at the close. A tangible memory of the session for the guest to keep.
