The teacher

Hi, I'm
Zey.

Maldivian · 200-hour certified · Founder of Salt to Soul

From doing to being

I didn't plan to become a yoga teacher. I planned to come back to myself. Teaching turned out to be the same thing.

Salt to Soul began the way most real things do — not as a business idea, but as a need. A need to move. To slow down. To find, in the middle of a very loud island city, somewhere quiet to breathe.

For 12 years I worked in Maldivian hospitality — holding space for other people, learning the smallest gestures of care.

Life brought its own teachings. Ones I did not choose but could not avoid. Through those years, I found my way back to stillness — and began to understand that slowing down is not a retreat from the world. It is a deeper way of meeting it.

Movement became part of that journey. What began as a way to inhabit my own body became a practice that helped me feel present, grounded, and genuinely at home in my own skin.

I trained in Bali. 200 hours immersed in asana, philosophy, anatomy, and breathwork. I came back to Malé carrying something I could not have planned: a clear sense of what this work is for.

Salt to Soul grew from that. Rooted in island life. Shaped by the ocean. Offered to those who are ready to return to themselves.

The symbol

The spiral is
the whole story.

The spiral is one of the oldest symbols humanity has ever drawn. It moves inward and outward at once — representing the winding journey we must take into ourselves if we are to truly know who we are.

Every time you step onto a mat, you begin that journey again. You spiral a little deeper. You return carrying something you didn't have before — more stillness, more presence, more of yourself.

That is Salt to Soul. Not a class. Not a programme. A practice of returning.

Salt — from the islands that shaped her.
Soul — what the practice gives back. The name. The logo. The whole idea.

Teaching philosophy

No performance required.
Just show up.

Zey's approach is grounded in alignment and permission. Alignment — because understanding how the body is designed to move protects it and deepens the practice. Permission — because most people arrive already carrying a story about what they can't do.

In her sessions you will not be asked to perform. You will be invited to explore. To stay with sensation. To notice what happens when you stop running from stillness and let it arrive.

What guides the practice

What Salt to Soul
stands for.

01
Accessibility

Every session is designed to be entered at any level. The person who has never done yoga and the person who has practiced for years are both welcome. Neither is more valid than the other.

02
Honesty

In the body, in the room, in the teaching. Zey guides from a place of presence, not performance. She teaches what she knows, acknowledges what she doesn't, and stays curious.

03
The Maldives

This practice belongs to these islands. Shaped by the ocean, the light, the heat, and the particular stillness you only find when water surrounds you on every side.
