From doing to being

Hi, I'm Zey.

Maldivian, through and through.

For over ten years, I worked in the Maldivian hospitality industry. It taught me how to care for people, stay calm under pressure, and appreciate the beauty in small moments. Throughout it all, the ocean was a constant — always moving, always returning to itself.

Over the years, life and the people I met challenged me in ways I never expected. Those experiences slowly brought me closer to myself. I learned to enjoy my own company, protect my peace, and appreciate the value of slowing down.

Yoga became part of that journey. What started as a way to move my body gradually became a practice that helped me feel more grounded, present, and connected.

Wanting to deepen my understanding, I travelled to Bali to complete my 200-hour Yoga Teacher Training. Becoming a certified yoga teacher gave me not only the knowledge to teach, but also a deeper appreciation for the philosophy and practice of yoga beyond the mat.

Salt to Soul grew from that experience. Inspired by island life and the rhythm of the ocean, Salt to Soul is about creating space to slow down, breathe deeply, and reconnect — with yourself, with nature, and with the present moment.
