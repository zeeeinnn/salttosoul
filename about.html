From doing to being

I didn't plan to become a yoga teacher. I planned to come back to myself. Teaching turned out to be the same thing.

For 12 years I worked in Maldivian hospitality — learning how to hold space for people, stay composed under pressure, and find meaning in the smallest gestures of care.

Life brought its own teachings. Through those years I found my way back to stillness, and began to understand that slowing down is not a retreat from the world. It is a deeper way of meeting it.

The Brand

The spiral is
the whole story.

The spiral is one of the oldest symbols humanity has ever drawn — found in cave art, carved into stone, coiled into shells. It represents movement that never stagnates. Growth that honours what came before.

It is not a circle. A circle loops back to the same point. The spiral returns — but always on a higher turn, carrying what it learned, releasing what it doesn't need.

This is the practice. This is the brand. Every class, every breath, every session is one more turn of the spiral — inward, deeper, toward the truest version of you.

Salt — from the islands that shaped her.
Soul — what the practice gives back.

The Loop.

Your ego wants to stay where it's safe. The same morning. The same heaviness. The same story about who you are and what you're capable of. The loop is familiar — it feels like home, even when it isn't. You circle and circle and never quite arrive anywhere new.

Repeating the same cycle. This is what the egoic mind finds comfortable — it's all it knows.

The Spiral.

Your intuition calls you somewhere different. Back — but changed. Each return is on a higher turn. You carry what you've learned, release what no longer serves you. Growth is not linear. It spirals — inward, deeper, toward the truest version of you.

Making gradual changes that lead toward ongoing evolution. Each turn brings you closer to who you are becoming.

Every session

The spiral
turns inward.

The first turn Arrive. Breathe.

You step onto the mat carrying everything — the inbox, the worry, the noise. You don't leave it at the door. You bring it with you. And then you breathe.

The second turn Move. Remember.

Your body knows things your mind has forgotten. Movement unlocks them. By the time you're in warrior two, something has already shifted.

The third turn Still. Listen.

Savasana is not an ending. It is the deepest point of the spiral. The noise has quieted. In that quiet — something true is waiting.

The centre You. Present. Whole.

You are your own safe space. You were, all along. The practice simply clears the path back to that knowing. You leave carrying that centre — until next time, when the spiral turns again.
