The teacher

Hi,
I'm
Zey.

From doing to being

I didn't plan to become a yoga teacher. I planned to come back to myself. Teaching turned out to be the same thing.

For 12 years I worked in Maldivian hospitality — learning how to hold space for people, stay composed under pressure, and find meaning in the smallest gestures of care.

Life brought its own teachings. Through those years I found my way back to stillness, and began to understand that slowing down is not a retreat from the world. It is a deeper way of meeting it.

I trained in Bali. I taught on a private island. I came home to Malé and built the practice I always wanted to find.

Zey · Founder, Salt to Soul
200-hour certified,
Yoga Union · Bali 2026
The symbol

The spiral is
the whole story.

The spiral is one of the oldest symbols humanity has ever drawn. It moves inward and outward — representing the journey we must take into ourselves if we are to truly know who we are.

Every time you step onto a mat, you begin that journey again. You spiral a little deeper. You return carrying something you didn't have before.

Salt — from the islands that shaped her.

How it all began

Everything happened
in one year.

01
200-Hour Teacher Training
Yoga Union · Bali, 2026

A full residential immersion — asana, alignment, breathwork, pranayama, yoga philosophy, anatomy, and the art of holding space. The month in Bali changed how she understood her body and what it means to guide another person through theirs.

02
Resident Yoga Teacher
Soneva Secret · Maldives, 2026

A two-week teaching residency at one of the Maldives' most exclusive private island resorts — daily sessions overlooking the Indian Ocean, and the deepening ability to meet people exactly where they are.

03
Founder & Lead Teacher
Salt to Soul · Malé, 2026 —

Sunrise yoga on the rooftop. Full moon gatherings. Private sessions. Dance meditation. All of it rooted in these islands, offered with full presence. The practice she had always wanted to find — now open to everyone.
