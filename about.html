The teacher

About Zey

Maldivian. 200-hour certified. Founder of Salt to Soul. Teacher of yoga and dance meditation.

Her story

A Maldivian woman who chose movement.

Zey grew up in the Maldives — surrounded by the ocean, by the rhythm of island life, and by a deep, instinctive pull toward the body as a place of truth. For a long time she didn't have a word for it. She just knew she felt most like herself when she was moving.

Yoga found her the way it finds most people — at a moment of searching. What started as a physical practice became something much deeper: a way of listening. Of slowing down long enough to hear what was actually there.

When she decided to train as a teacher, she went to Bali. 200 hours. One month. Immersed in the study of asana, philosophy, anatomy, and the immense responsibility of guiding another person's body. She came back a different version of herself — not because Bali changed her, but because she finally had a framework for who she already was.

Salt to Soul is the practice she has been building since. It is rooted in the Maldives, shaped by the ocean and the sky, and offered with the belief that movement is available to everyone — not just people who look a certain way on a mat.

Teaching philosophy

No performance required.

Zey's approach to teaching is grounded in two things: alignment and permission. Alignment — because understanding how the body is designed to move protects it and deepens the practice. Permission — because most people arrive on a mat already carrying a story about what they can't do.

In her classes, you will not be asked to perform. You will be invited to explore. To stay with sensation. To notice what happens when you stop running from stillness and simply let it arrive.

This applies to both yoga and dance meditation. In movement, there is no right or wrong. There is only what is honest.

What guides the practice

What Salt to Soul stands for.

01
Accessibility
Every session is designed to be entered at any level. The person who has never done yoga and the person who has practiced for years are both welcome. Neither is more valid than the other.
02
Honesty
In the body, in the room, in the teaching. Zey does not guide from a place of performance. She teaches what she knows, acknowledges what she doesn't, and stays curious.
03
The Maldives
This practice belongs to these islands. It is shaped by the ocean, the light, the heat, and the particular stillness you only find when you're surrounded by water on every side.
