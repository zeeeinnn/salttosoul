Who we are

A practice rooted in the Maldives.

Salt to Soul is a wellness practice built around the belief that movement is not a performance — it is a returning. To the breath. To the body. To yourself.

Founded by Zey, a 200-hour certified yoga teacher and Maldivian movement practitioner, Salt to Soul offers sessions that are equally accessible to someone who has never been on a mat and someone who has been on one for years.
